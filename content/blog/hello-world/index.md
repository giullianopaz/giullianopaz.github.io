This is my new website, rebuilt from the ground up with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and the [Blowfish](https://blowfish.page/) theme.

## What to expect

I'll be writing about:

- **Python & Django** — Backend engineering, architecture patterns, and real-world solutions
- **Developer Tools** — The terminal, editors, and workflows that keep me productive
- **Open Source** — Projects I build and contribute to
- **Career** — Lessons learned as a software engineer

## The stack

This site is powered by:

- **Hugo** — The world's fastest static site generator
- **Blowfish** — A Tailwind CSS-based theme with profile layouts, card views, and i18n
- **GitHub Pages** — Free, reliable hosting
- **JetBrains Mono** — The font that ties everything together with an Omarchy-inspired aesthetic
