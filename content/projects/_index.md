## Featured Projects

### 📚 [Homeschooling Platform — Evidence-Based Early Childhood Education](/games/aula-em-casa/)

An interactive, slide-based lesson platform designed for 60-minute daily home lessons (divided into four dynamic 15-minute micro-blocks), grounded in cognitive science and global benchmarks:

- **Singapore Math (CPA Approach)**: Concrete ➔ Pictorial ➔ Abstract progression, perceptual subitising, and Number Bonds (part-part-whole relationships and the *Make 10* strategy).
- **Synthetic Phonics & Onomatopoeia Method (Dra. Sandra Puliezi)**: Cumulative phoneme-grapheme correspondences, articulatory mouth observation, and strictly decodable Portuguese vocabulary.
- **Simultaneous Two-Age Differentiation**: Synchronized dual tracks for **Frantiesco (4yo)** (phonological awareness and sensory manipulatives) and **Giuseppe (5yo)** (word decoding, stroke calligraphy, and symbolic equations).
- **Consolidation & Japanese MEXT Routines**: Daily calendar math, tidy-up challenges, and Rosenshine's 80% mastery Friday checklist persisted in `localStorage`.
- **Curricular Scope**: Weeks 1 to 9 complete (45 daily lesson modules, over 700 interactive presentation slides).
